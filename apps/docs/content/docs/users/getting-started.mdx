This page walks you from a fresh browser tab to a working account on
Mikoto. The flow is the same on the hosted instance at
[mikoto.io](https://mikoto.io) and on any self-hosted server.

## 1. Create an account

Open the client and choose **Sign up**. You'll be asked for:

- An **email address** — used for password recovery and notifications.
- A **password** — pick something unique; Mikoto stores only a hash.
- A **display name** — the friendly name shown next to your messages.

After submitting, check your inbox for a verification email. Until
you click the link, account features that involve other people
(joining spaces, sending DMs) are limited.

If you're on a self-hosted dev instance, verification emails land in
[MailHog](http://localhost:35109) instead of your real inbox.

## 2. Claim a handle

A **handle** is your public, human-readable identity. It looks like:

```
you.mikoto.io
```

The first part is yours to pick; the second part is the domain of
your home instance (`mikoto.io` for the hosted product, or whatever
the admin configured for a self-hosted server).

You can also use a **custom domain** if you control its DNS — see
[Handles & identity](/users/handles) for the verification flow.

## 3. Find people to talk to

Two ways to start a conversation:

- **Add a friend.** Open the **Friends** panel, search by handle,
  and send a friend request. Once they accept, you can DM them.
- **Join a space.** Spaces are invite-based by default. Paste an
  invite link or code into the **Join space** dialog.

If you want to start your own community instead of joining one, see
[Creating a space](/users/spaces).

## 4. Pick your notifications

By default Mikoto pings you for direct mentions and DMs only. Open
**Settings → Notifications** to enable:

- **Web push** — desktop notifications even when the tab is closed.
- **Per-channel overrides** — mute noisy channels or boost important
  ones to "all messages".
