---
title: Handles & identity
description: Default-domain handles, custom-domain handles, and how verification works.
---

A **handle** is your public, human-readable identity on Mikoto. It
looks like a domain name:

```
hayley.mikoto.io
alice.example.com
```

Handles replace the discriminator system used by older chat
platforms (`alice#1234`). They're easier to share, more memorable,
and let you bring your own domain.

## Default-domain handles

When you sign up, the server vends you a handle under its default
domain — `you.mikoto.io` on the hosted product, or whatever the
self-hosted server is configured to use.

Picking one is a single step inside the **Settings → Handle** panel.
Handles are unique within a domain; if your first choice is taken,
the panel will tell you.

You can change a default-domain handle later, but the old one is
released back to the pool after a short cool-down.

## Custom-domain handles

If you own a domain (e.g. `example.com`), you can use it as your
handle's suffix. The verification flow proves to the server that you
control the domain's DNS:

1. From **Settings → Handle → Custom domain**, enter the domain.
2. The server gives you a signed attestation string.
3. Add the attestation as a **TXT record** on the domain.
4. Click **Verify**.
5. The server looks up the TXT record over DNS-over-HTTPS and
   checks the signature.

Once verified, your handle becomes `you.example.com`. You can use
the bare apex (`example.com`) or any subdomain you like.

Verification is a one-time step per domain. You can keep the TXT
record in place permanently, or remove it after verification — the
server caches the result.

## Why DID-style handles?

Handles double as a **decentralized identifier** in the longer term.
Because the verification is cryptographic and DNS-anchored, the same
identity can move between Mikoto servers without losing its public
name. (This portability is in progress — for now, handles are pinned
to the server that vended them.)

## Switching handles

You can change your handle from **Settings → Handle**. Changing it:

- Updates your public profile immediately.
- Does **not** retroactively change your handle on past messages —
  those keep showing your name at the time of sending.
- Doesn't break invite links or friend relationships.
