title: Spaces
description: Top-level communities — joining, creating, and managing them.
---

A **space** is the top-level container for a community on Mikoto.
Spaces hold channels, members, roles, and invites. They're roughly
equivalent to a Discord server or a Slack workspace.

## Joining a space

Spaces are invite-only by default. To join one you need an **invite
code** or **invite link** from someone already inside.

1. Open the space picker (left sidebar) and click **Join space**.
2. Paste the invite code or link.
3. Confirm. The space appears in your sidebar immediately.

Invites can be revoked by the space's admins, so if a link stops
working ask the inviter to mint a fresh one.

## Creating a space

Anyone with a verified account can create a space:

1. Click the **+** at the bottom of the space picker.
2. Choose a name and (optionally) an icon.
3. Confirm.

You become the **owner** of the new space — the role with full
permissions. From here you can:

- Create channels (text, voice, or document).
- Invite people via the **Members → Invite** panel.
- Define roles to delegate permissions.

## Roles and permissions

Permissions in Mikoto are role-based. A role bundles a set of
permission bits — "send messages", "manage channels", "kick members",
and so on — and gets assigned to members. A member's effective
permissions are the union of the bits on all the roles they hold.

The default roles in a new space:

- **Owner** — full control, including deleting the space.
- **Admin** — manage channels, roles, invites, members.
- **Member** — read and write in channels.

Owners and admins can create new roles from the **Settings → Roles**
panel. Each role is a colored label and a permission bitset.

## Channels in a space

Channels are the rooms inside a space. A fresh space starts with
one general text channel. Add more from **Settings → Channels**:

- **Text** — threaded chat. See [Channels & messaging](/users/messaging).
- **Voice** — drop-in voice and video. See [Voice & video](/users/voice).
- **Document** — collaborative wiki page. See [Wiki documents](/users/documents).

Channels can be reordered, renamed, and grouped into categories at
any time.

## Bans and moderation

Removing people from a space comes in two flavors:

- **Kick** — removes the member, but they can rejoin with a fresh
  invite.
- **Ban** — removes the member and blocks them from rejoining. You
  can lift a ban from **Settings → Bans**.

Both require the "moderate members" permission.

## Leaving or deleting a space

- **Leave**: right-click the space in your sidebar → **Leave space**.
  You lose access immediately and need a new invite to come back.
- **Delete**: only the owner can delete a space, from
  **Settings → Space → Delete**. This is permanent — all channels,
  messages, and documents in the space are removed.
