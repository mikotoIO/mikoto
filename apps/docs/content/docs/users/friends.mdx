title: Friends & DMs
description: Friend requests, blocks, and direct messages.

Friends in Mikoto are a separate layer from spaces. Adding someone
as a friend lets you DM them and see their presence, even if you
don't share any spaces.

## Adding a friend

Open the **Friends** panel and click **Add friend**. Enter their
handle — for example `alice.mikoto.io` or `bob.example.com`.

The other person receives a **friend request** they can accept,
decline, or ignore. They won't be DM'd or otherwise notified beyond
the request itself.

## Friend states

A relationship between two accounts is one of:

- **None** — no relationship.
- **Outgoing request** — you've sent a request; they haven't
  responded.
- **Incoming request** — they sent you a request.
- **Friends** — both sides accepted.
- **Blocked** — you've blocked them. They cannot send you friend
  requests, DMs, or see your presence.

The **Friends** panel groups everyone by state so you can quickly
see who's pending, who's online, and who you've blocked.

## Direct messages

Once you're friends with someone you can open a DM channel from
their profile or from the **Direct messages** section of the
sidebar.

- **1:1 DMs** work like a private text channel between you and one
  other person.
- **Group DMs** let you add multiple friends to a shared channel.

DMs support the same Markdown, mentions, replies, reactions, and
attachments as space channels — see
[Channels & messaging](/users/messaging).

## Blocking

Right-click a user → **Block**. Blocking is one-sided:

- They can no longer DM you or send friend requests.
- Their messages in shared spaces are hidden from your view.
- They aren't notified that they've been blocked.

You can unblock from **Settings → Privacy → Blocked users**.

## Presence

Friends see each other's presence state — **online**, **idle**,
**do not disturb**, or **offline**. Set yours from the user menu in
the top-left of the client. "Do not disturb" suppresses all push
notifications until you switch back.
